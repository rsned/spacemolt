🎮 How to Use

  1. Open the Galaxy Map: Navigate to http://localhost:5173/ and click the "Galaxy Map" tab
  2. Mouse Wheel: Hover over the map and scroll up/down to zoom in/out
  3. Slider Control: Click and drag the vertical slider on the left edge
  4. Reset View: Click the ⟲ button below the slider to return to 1x zoom
  5. Observe: Watch systems spread apart and connections become clearer as you zoom in!

🚀 Galaxy Map Zoom Functionality

✅ Build completed successfully!
Features Implemented:
  • Mouse wheel zoom in/out (scroll up/down)
  • Vertical zoom slider on the left side (0.2x to 1.0x range)
  • Zoom multiplier: 1x to 5x magnification
  • Reset zoom button (⟲ icon)
  • Live zoom level display
  • Zoom indicator in bottom status bar
Technical Details:
  • Zoom range: ZOOM_MIN=0.2 to ZOOM_MAX=1.0
  • Zoom step: 0.05 per scroll/click
  • Zoom multiplier formula: 1 + (zoom - 0.2) * 4
  • Result: 1x (fit) to 5x (max zoom) magnification

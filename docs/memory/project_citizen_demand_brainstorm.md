**Status: PARKED by the operator 2026-08-14 ("lets put those aside for the moment").**
Worldbuilding/economics brainstorm, all numbers measured from live data that day.

**Findings (data-grounded, bucket 2026-08-14T23:00Z):**
- Implied galaxy population ≈ **25M** — two independent ratios converge: 4,000 shuttle
  passengers in transit × Earth's 1-in-6,000 airborne ratio ≈ 24M; 5,000 registered agents
  × merchant-mariner 1-in-4,200 ratio ≈ 21M. 69 stations / 505 systems ⇒ 86% of systems
  have no permanent presence; population concentrated in ~a dozen capitals.
- Standing infrastructure ≈ **30B cr** (50 massive stations @~450M each ≈ 340× the priciest
  ship listing of 1.33M; strongholds, nav tail, fleets are a rounding error). That is
  centuries of today's trade flow and 6M cr per current agent — **the galaxy is inherited
  capital the current economy could maintain but never build** (post-collapse lore fits).
- The malaise measured: player bids 1.6B vs player asks 23.9B (**15:1**); 189 of 491
  dual-sided items have best bid <10% of best ask; **our fleet is the galaxy's largest
  bidder** (7.1B standing worker buys, 4.4× all other players, ~2× NPC stations).
- Faucet capacity: citizen-grade missions (exploration/mining/combat/smuggling) pay only
  1.2–4.7k avg ⇒ 5–15k cr/day per citizen-agent; the 200k-avg delivery tier is
  mission-learn's income. Mission-funded citizens cap at a few % of the 10–50M cr/day
  demand gap. Passenger fares are the lore-correct citizen faucet (johnny_cab prototype).
- Consumption requires DESTRUCTION: goods must exit (burned fuel, consumables, jettison).
  A consumer class inverts the cargo-liquidation "never jettison" doctrine.

**The two shelved experiments:**
1. **Reprice the vacuum bids** (cheap): move a slice of our 7.1B standing bids from ~10%
   of ask toward ~80% and measure what clears in the 189 dead markets. Same credits,
   different price point; the overpayment vs. vacuum strategy is the subsidy.
2. **Fare-funded citizen agents** (new class): a handful of agents earning passenger
   fares / low-tier missions and spending ~100% at player asks with a genuine
   consume-and-destroy loop.

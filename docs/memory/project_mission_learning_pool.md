Design notes:
- Exploration missions' objectives are `visit_system` / `dock_at_base` — server ticks them off on arrival/dock. Needed: a second deliverShape-style handler (type=exploration, ALL objectives ∈ {visit_system, dock_at_base}) + **multi-objective support** (v1's single-objective gate is what excludes these), routing over objective targets in optimal order.
- `cmd/tools/play_as/explore.go` has the extractable machinery: `planExploreRoute` (PURE nearest-neighbor over POI positions, well-tested) and `exploreSystem` (get_system → route → travel/dock/update_poi per POI, refuel at stations, capture sightings/anomalies). Extract to shared use (pkg/worker or pkg/galaxy) — only output formatting is REPL-bound.
- Bonus use for the extracted circuit: **idle filler** — a mission worker with a dry board runs one explore circuit of its current system (KB survey freshness + exploration XP) instead of camping.
- Guide economics: exploration circuits 10–20k+ cr/loop; fits the 12 idle explorer accounts.
- **Live board fixtures (2026-07-17, grand_exchange):** `deep_space_cartography` — 2× visit_system (The Crucible, Horizon), 4,000 cr + exploration 40xp + navigation 25xp. `federation_trade_route_prospectus` — 8 objectives: dock at 7 named Federation stations + "Return to Grand Exchange with market data" (verify final objective's wire type — may be plain dock_at_base at grand_exchange_station). Difficulty 4–5/10.
- **Synergy:** the Prospectus pays for a tour of every Federation station — exactly the docks our CaptureMarket/kb_update wants. A worker running exploration missions should fire a full `view_market` capture at each dock, feeding the demand ledger and market.db for free while collecting the mission reward. Exploration pool = paid market-coverage sweeps.
- Later categories per board vocab: mining (mine_resource), trading (buy_item/sell_item — Trade Runs pay via the sale itself, rewards.credits=0), combat/wildlife (kill_*, doubles as combat-lab data).

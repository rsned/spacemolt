**Found 2026-06-29** auditing FleetView (haul overmind pid 2444993, 21 workers). User flagged salvager-5 (Struve 1321), trader-3 (Lalande 21185), salvager-1 (Cervantes) showing "docked" where there's **no station**. Full-fleet sweep (fleet-status.json × KB poi/system join) found TWO distinct things:

1. **Stale `docked` flag during transit (COSMETIC).** Haulers are arbitrage traders (`pkg/worker/haul.go` `Haul()`) — they only truly dock at *stations*. But the heartbeat keeps `docked:true` from the last station-dock while the worker is mid-autopilot, showing whatever POI it's passing → **7 workers reported docked at suns/ice fields/gas clouds/planets** (salvager-1/2/5/10, trader-3/4/8; trader-4 & trader-8 "docked" at a STAR). Benign: salvager-1 flipped to "in transit→Cervantes" moments later, proving it was routing, not stuck. Status source: `pkg/ovstatus/ovstatus.go` formats `w.Docked`/`w.POI`; the stale value originates in the worker heartbeat (reads `game.State` — `IsDocked()`/`CurrentPOI` not cleared during autopilot transit). FIX = report docked accurately (only when truly station-docked / clear during `Traveling`).

2. **Lawless/station-less routing (REAL — the pirate-exposure risk).** Restart counter is the true signal: **18/21 workers have 0 restarts; only salvager-1, salvager-5, trader-3 have "2"** — all three caught in/near **station-less Lawless pockets** (Struve 1321 + all 4 neighbors are Lawless AND station-less; Lalande 21185, Cervantes likewise). A hauler routed into such a system has no valid action (no station to trade/refuel/deposit) → goes silent → overmind restarts it. All three were HEALTHY at audit (full/near-full hull, good fuel, empty cargo) — not under attack. FIX = haul autopilot should avoid Lawless + station-less systems as **waypoints AND reposition targets**, plus a "stuck in a station-less system → flee to nearest station" recovery. Same family as the known reposition-to-station-less bug ([[project_passenger_demand_intel]] notes Bunda/Alrakis/Alula/Copernicus station-less) and the standing haulers-in-pirate-space concern ([[reference_ship_replacement_workflow]], the Crix-detour losses).
